Phan Nguyen

Born and raised in Hanoi, Phan Nguyen graduated with a degree in Film Directing from the Hanoi Academy of Theatre and Cinema in 2017. With nearly a decade of experience spanning commercials, music videos, short films, and television, Phan brings a deep-rooted passion for cinema to every project he takes on.

He is drawn to the quiet power of visual storytelling: finding meaning in subtle gestures, honest emotions, and the spaces between words. Through every frame, Phan strives to tell stories that feel simple yet deeply felt.

Stories that linger long after the screen fades to black.
